# Claims and Evidence Matrix

| Claim | Evidence | Experiment/Phase | Statistic | Strength of Evidence | Potential Reviewer Objection | How We Address It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High internal performance is an illusion of generalization.** | WESAD AUC=0.964 drops to Dataset B AUC=0.423. | Study Comp 1 & 2 (Phases 1 & 6) | AUC $\Delta = -0.541$ | High | "WESAD is just a bad dataset to learn from." | We explicitly acknowledge the difference in datasets; the claim is simply that internal validation metrics (LOSO) cannot predict external robustness. |
| **Accelerometer drives the largest cross-dataset distribution shift.** | ACC Z mean Cohen's $d \approx -1.47$. Removal of ACC increases Absolute AUC from 0.423 to 0.540. | Study Comp 3 (Phase 6) | Cohen's $d$; AUC $\Delta = +0.117$ | High | "Perhaps the sensor was just worn differently." | We state explicitly that ACC captures experimental protocol (movement differences) rather than physiological stress, which is exactly why it shifts so much. |
| **Subject-specific baseline normalization rescues generalization.** | Relative normalization without ACC achieves AUC=1.000 vs. Absolute without ACC at AUC=0.540. | Study Comp 4 (Phase 6 & 10) | AUC $\Delta = +0.460$ | High | "1.000 AUC is impossible in the real world." | We heavily contextualize the 1.000 AUC as an artifact of the finite, laboratory-controlled Dataset B cohort. We claim it "substantially improves transfer", not "solves stress detection perfectly." |
| **The representation transferred is stable and equivalent.** | SHAP attribution correlates highly across datasets. | Study Comp 5 (Phase 12) | Spearman $\rho = 0.9527$, Jaccard=1.000 | Strong | "SHAP doesn't prove biology." | We explicitly state we are measuring *model representation equivalence*, NOT discovering universal biological causal markers. |
| **The performance increase is statistically robust within the cohort.** | Subject-level bootstrap and permutation tests confirm separation. | Phase 3 & 11 | $p=0.0000$ (permutation), non-overlapping subject margins | High | "Overlapping windows inflate statistics." | We aggregate at the subject level before running inferential statistics, specifically avoiding window-level inflation. |
